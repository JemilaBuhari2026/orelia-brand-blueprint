# Hey! You Wellness — Brand Website

A showcase website that puts the rebranded Hey! You world online: story, products, African ingredients, wellness journal and contact. No online checkout for now.

## On the colour combination

Mauve purple + burnt orange + chocolate brown + warm cream is a strong, unusual pairing for a wellness food brand. Two honest notes:

- Mauve alone reads calm and beauty-adjacent rather than appetising. Food brands need warmth, so burnt orange and chocolate brown must do real work — not just accents on buttons, but in photography, product cards and campaign blocks.
- Of the sampled purples, the deeper one (#7F6276) is the safest hero: it holds the Orélia mood but has enough depth for readable white text on packaging and screens. The palest sample looks washed out on phones.

Recommendation: hero purple #7F6276, burnt orange #C86A1E as the energy accent, chocolate brown #4A2C1E for text and depth, warm cream #F3EBE3 as the main page background. Easy to swap if you prefer another sample.

## The logo

The trademarked logo stays exactly as it is — same shape, type, wording, proportions. Only the purple fill is replaced with the chosen brand purple. I'll produce a recoloured version plus a version that works on dark/purple backgrounds, and show them before they go live.

## Pages

- **Home** — brand promise, hero product moment, what makes Hey! You different, product family preview, ingredient story, journal teaser, newsletter signup.
- **Our Story** — purpose, vision, mission, values, founder story, brand philosophy.
- **Products** — the six concepts (Furafrost, Mood Bars, Crunch Sticks, Cocoa Boost, Hey! You Superfoods, Naija Cola) as a family grid, each with its own detail page: proposition, taste, hero ingredients, how to enjoy, benefit.
- **African Superfoods** — ingredient library with heritage and wellness notes.
- **Wellness Journal** — article listing across the content clusters (gut health, food × mood, superfoods, snacking, recipes), with a few written starter articles.
- **Corporate & Gifting** — B2B, wellness programmes, hospitality, seasonal gifting.
- **Contact** — enquiry details and a simple message form.

## Look and feel

Warm cream canvas, generous space, editorial layout. Serif display headings for premium warmth, clean sans for body. Purple as the recognisable field colour on hero and feature bands, orange for calls to action and campaign flashes, brown for text. Soft rounded product cards, subtle fades on scroll. No clinical white, no childish health-food styling, no stock-photo wellness clichés.

Placeholder product and lifestyle imagery is generated in the brand palette so the site looks finished; swap in real photography when it's shot.

## Copy

All written from your branding document — positioning language, values, product descriptions, ingredient stories, journal articles. Nothing invented about prices, certifications, nutrition figures or testimonials; those are left out until you supply them.

## Technical notes

- Static content-driven site, no backend needed for this phase. The newsletter and contact forms will be built as UI now and wired to a backend when you're ready to collect submissions.
- Brand colours and fonts defined once as design tokens so a colour change is one edit.
- Per-page titles, descriptions and social previews for search.
